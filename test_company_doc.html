2. Refund, Return & Lost Parcel Policy (Edge Case Section)

Customers may cancel a shipment free of charge ONLY before the courier scans the barcode at pickup. Once scanned, cancellation incurs a 15 MAD return-to-origin fee.

Lost or Damaged Claims: Must be submitted within 48 hours of expected delivery with unboxing video proof. Maximum compensation without extra insurance is 500 MAD, even if the item inside was worth 5,000 MAD (unless the merchant purchased Premium Shield at 2% of declared value).

Prohibited Items: Cash, jewelry, perishable fresh food, lithium batteries over 100Wh, and flammable liquids. Note: Perfumes under 100ml ARE allowed domestically if sealed in bubble wrap, despite what older brochures claimed.
